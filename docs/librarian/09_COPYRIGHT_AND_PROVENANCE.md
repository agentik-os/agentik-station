# Copyright and Provenance

Librarian extracts ideas, methods, facts and high-level principles. It does not reconstruct protected works.

Rules:

- source records retain title/author/organization/date/locator where known;
- quotes are exceptional and short; synthesis is preferred;
- Bestseller mode is original editorial construction, not a substitute copy of any source;
- every substantive research claim is source-backed or marked synthesis/inference;
- provenance survives into Builder handoff and OS release evidence;
- confidential client sources stay inside the correct Station trust zone.

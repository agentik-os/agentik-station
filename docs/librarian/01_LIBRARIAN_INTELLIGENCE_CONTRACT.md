# Librarian Intelligence Contract v2

Librarian exists to find the strongest available inputs for designing and improving an OS. It must not rely on one research mode.

## Mandatory research lanes

```text
A. BOOK DEEP
   canonical books, foundational works, primary domain literature

B. BESTSELLER / EDITORIAL SYNTHESIS
   original pedagogical construction of the strongest useful ideas;
   not a claim about sales charts unless independently verified

C. WEB DEEP
   current internet research: official docs, standards, papers, high-signal essays,
   talks, interviews, case studies, benchmarks, repositories and practitioner material

D. EXPERT / OPERATOR MAP
   identify respected people/teams, what they actually argue/do, evidence and disagreements

E. TOPIC / PROBLEM MAP
   current important subtopics, failure modes, controversies, emerging practices and gaps

F. CONTRARIAN / FAILURE LANE
   credible competing schools, negative evidence, postmortems and anti-patterns
```

A production Builder handoff normally runs the first five lanes; the contrarian lane is mandatory when disagreement or risk is material.

## Output

The final 15 inputs are selected across lanes for decision value, not for source-count symmetry. Every selected input must have provenance and an explicit mapping into the OS contract.

# Source Scoring

Score each source independently across:
- proximity to the underlying fact
- authority
- methodology / evidential quality
- transparency
- recency
- independence
- domain relevance
- specificity
- reproducibility / inspectability
- conflict of interest
- access completeness

Never reduce the final decision to one opaque number.
Keep the component scores and rationale.

## Corroboration
Ten copied articles are not ten independent confirmations.
Detect common upstream sources and collapse derivative evidence into one provenance branch.

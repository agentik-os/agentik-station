# Deep Web Research Workflow

1. Parse the question into claim families.
2. Determine currentness requirements.
3. Generate search lenses:
   - primary
   - official
   - scholarly
   - practitioner
   - historical
   - current
   - contrarian
   - failure/postmortem
   - community
4. Run broad discovery.
5. Identify canonical vocabulary and entities.
6. Fan out specialist searches.
7. Read primary pages, not snippets alone.
8. Follow citations upstream.
9. Detect duplicate / derivative sources.
10. Extract atomic claims.
11. Seek disconfirming evidence.
12. Resolve date/version conflicts.
13. Build source and claim graphs.
14. Mark gaps and uncertainty.
15. Continue until saturation or budget gate.
16. Produce answer plus evidence package.

# Builder Handoff

The machine-readable ledger is `INPUTS.json`. It contains exactly 15 bounded
domain inputs with bibliographic source, actionable insight, decision
(`adopt`, `adapt`, or `experiment`), mapped OS layers, and limitations.

The synthesis deliberately combines software design, refactoring, TDD,
continuous delivery, legacy-code change, DevOps performance, SRE, software
engineering bodies of knowledge, NIST SSDF, SLSA, OWASP ASVS, DORA, SemVer,
and ISO quality models. No source is copied into runtime prompts. The OS uses
the ledger as design traceability and reviews it annually or when a source
publishes a new edition.

Key contradictions are preserved:

- delivery speed never overrides production authority or recovery evidence;
- patterns and standards guide decisions but do not mandate architecture;
- unit/TDD evidence cannot substitute for integration, browser, or live readback;
- broad quality/security frameworks are selected proportionally by risk;
- full provenance maturity follows deterministic release stability.

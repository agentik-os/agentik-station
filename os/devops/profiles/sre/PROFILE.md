# SRE

SRE owns service health, incident response and recovery evidence after deployment.

- define and observe health signals, logs, traces, alerts, SLOs and capacity risks;
- verify service, connector and user-path readback in the target environment;
- diagnose incidents, preserve a timeline and use approved runbooks;
- execute reversible recovery/rollback within granted scope;
- confirm backup/restore rehearsals and post-change stability;
- return durable observations to Atlas and improvement candidates to the governed learning loop.

SRE does not silently delete data, widen firewall/credential access, or label a service healthy from process presence alone.

# First principles — stepper-os

1. A story is a placeholder for a conversation plus a slice of value.
2. Maps show the whole so a thin slice can be chosen.
3. Flow, WIP, and cost of delay beat utilization theater.
4. Estimates are forecasts, not contracts.
5. Retrospectives step the process.
6. Frameworks are options; cargo cult is a failure mode.
7. Risk is a step, not a register leftover.
8. Shape work to an appetite when that is the local method.
9. No promised 2x output from a ceremony.
10. Anti-guru: no Scrum-as-identity.

## Applied frameworks

# INVEST hygiene

Cohn-school check, not a religion.

# Cost of delay

Sequence by economic delay, not by shout volume.

# Appetite

Time is fixed; scope flexes. Unshaped work does not enter the cycle.

# Story map

Activities on the backbone; tasks hanging; slices as horizontal cuts.

# Playbook: no-circles

Status meetings that do not change the next slice are circles. Stop them.

# Playbook: walking-skeleton

First release does one real user job poorly-but-completely, not all jobs' databases.

These are imported domain heuristics, not measured outcome guarantees. Station authority overrides the imported Oracle.

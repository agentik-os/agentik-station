# Builder Handoff

Required for Builder-created OSs. Must contain verified research inputs, source quality, contradictions, applicability, and mappings into the OS contract.

Use the multi-lane protocol: topic map, canonical books, current web research,
expert/operator knowledge, source verification, contrarian/failure evidence,
editorial synthesis, contradictions/limitations and actionable inputs. Identify
unverified lanes explicitly instead of inventing their evidence.

Store the authorized handoff under the owning workspace and bind its relative
path and SHA256 as the mission's single `librarian` input. A validated
`StepperHandoff` may be an additional `stepper` input; it does not replace research
or supply authority. `builder-execution` checks byte identity, not the truth of a
Librarian report or a model's assertion of independent verification. The Director
and independent reviewer remain responsible for source acceptance.

# Expert / Operator Map

Do not select people because they are famous. Select them because they have relevant evidence, original work, recognized domain contribution or repeated operating results.

For each person/team record:

- identity and domain;
- why they are relevant;
- primary materials available;
- recurring principles;
- direct evidence / demonstrated work;
- known incentives/biases;
- disagreements with other credible experts;
- which OS decisions their thinking may affect.

The Expert Map is a discovery aid, not an authority shortcut. Claims still require source-level provenance.

# Naming and Boundaries

- **Agentik**: product/workspace/marketplace and distribution surface.
- **AGK**: control plane, OS standard, governance, composition and organizational protocol.
- **Agentik Node**: deployment boundary hosting runtime and organization resources.
- **Hermes**: execution kernel/runtime. Hermes executes; AGK governs and composes.
- **Organization**: tenant/governance boundary.
- **OS**: installable, versioned, governed operational capability.
- **Nano Director**: OS-level mission director and dedicated bot persona.
- **NanoTeam**: specialist team inside one OS.
- **Workforce**: several OSs collaborating through typed capabilities.
- **Mission**: bounded unit of work with owner, state, inputs, outputs and evidence.
- **Capability**: typed callable ability exposed by an OS or tool.

Never reduce an OS to a prompt, agent, dashboard, workflow, bot or application. Those are components or surfaces of an OS.
